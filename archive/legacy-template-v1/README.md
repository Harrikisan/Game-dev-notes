## Why it was retired

- Output narrated the recording ("what the instructor said, at what timestamp") instead of
  teaching the subject.
- Timestamps polluted the study material instead of living in a dedicated source-reference file.
- No code-example discipline, no activity categorization, no concept-status metadata, no
  cumulative knowledge graph.
